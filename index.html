Empowering Ghanaian Retail & Business Management

Azigizaro Enterprise Shop Manager Suite is an all-in-one Point-of-Sale (POS), inventory tracker, and sales management solution tailored specifically for micro and small businesses in Ghana. Operate seamlessly offline, track sales in Ghanaian Cedis (GH₵), print instant PDF receipts, and sync live across devices.

Why Choose Azigizaro Enterprise Shop Manager Suite?

🛒 Offline POS Register

Ring up customers effortlessly with cart controls, dynamic stock depletion, auto-calculated totals in GH₵, and instantaneous PDF receipt generation.

📦 Smart Inventory Control

Keep precise tabs on item stock levels. Automatically alerts you on low or out-of-stock items, complete with PIN-protected edit controls to secure inventory.

📲 Integrated Mobile Money

Seamlessly accepts activations via Paystack supporting MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, and AT Money, ensuring instant Ghanaian currency processing.

🔄 Multi-Device Cloud Sync

Connect multiple store terminals using custom Sync Keys powered by Firebase Realtime Database. Track transactions from home or the counter simultaneously.

👥 Customer Directory

Maintain your buyer database with names and phone numbers. Assign transactions directly to walk-in or loyal customers for better transaction history.

📜 Financial Logs & Exporting

View daily grouped revenue totals and export comprehensive CSV spreadsheets for accounting, auditing, and tax preparation with one click.
